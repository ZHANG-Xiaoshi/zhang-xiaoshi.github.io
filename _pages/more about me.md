Research is the center of this site, but I also like keeping a few human signals visible. I grew up in Northeast Asia and am now building my PhD life in Southeast Asia. My recent work thinks about how robots can make sense of the physical world through touch, while my broader curiosity also extends to AI agents, public writing, and embodied practice.

## Thinking Style

I identify with the INTP temperament: analytical, exploratory, and drawn to systems that hide their real structure beneath the surface.

## PhD Journey and Agents

During the PhD process, I have become increasingly interested in AI agents: not only as tools for automation, but as new research companions that can change how we read, code, design experiments, write, and reflect.

## Public Writing and Media

Beyond Research

A more personal layer around the technical work: INTP-style systems thinking, a Northeast Asia to Southeast Asia PhD journey, public writing, agent curiosity, movement practice, and music.

Thinking Style

I identify with the INTP temperament: analytical, exploratory, and drawn to systems that hide their real structure beneath the surface. That is part of why tactile robotics feels natural to me. Contact, force, deformation, control, and learning are all invisible until the right sensing and modeling framework makes them legible.

PhD Journey and Agents

I come from Northeast Asia and am now building my research life in Southeast Asia. During the PhD process, I have become increasingly interested in AI agents: not only as tools for automation, but as new research companions that can change how we read, code, design experiments, write, and reflect.

Public Writing and Media

During my undergraduate years I made self-media content, which trained me to explain ideas, sense audience attention, and turn scattered experience into narrative. I am now experimenting with a more refined Xiaohongshu account around PhD reflections, research taste, and the lived process of technical growth.

Movement and Martial Practice

I like sports that involve balance, body control, and timing: snowboarding, ice skating, unicycle riding, drift boards, and Chinese martial arts. I also studied martial arts when I was younger, and I keep an interest in Tai Chi Plum Blossom Mantis Boxing and qigong.
